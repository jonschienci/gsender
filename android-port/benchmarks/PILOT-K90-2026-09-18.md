# K90 benchmark — Build 45 — 18 September 2026

Tested the installed `1.6.4-android.45-node24-prototype` APK on K90_ROW, Android 16, with 7.61 GiB physical RAM reported by Android. Portrait orientation, visualizer enabled, tablet charging through the Mac. No physical CNC was connected. No app build or app/UI code changes were made.

## Results

| Workload | Size | Result | Commands | Active streaming rate |
| --- | ---: | --- | ---: | ---: |
| contour-128KiB.nc | 128 KiB | Full ordered checksum PASS; no RX overflow | 3,978 | 500.5 lines/s |
| relief-1024KiB.nc | 1 MiB | Full ordered checksum PASS; no RX overflow | 32,876 | 499.5 lines/s |
| arcs-128KiB.nc | 128 KiB | Full ordered checksum PASS; no RX overflow | 3,243 | 500.5 lines/s |
| relief-5120KiB.nc | 5 MiB | Full ordered checksum PASS; no RX overflow | 162,404 | 497.0 lines/s |
| Dense XYZ relief sample | 20 MiB | Loaded; streamed for 64.0 s; intentionally stopped | 31,945 acknowledged | 499.0 lines/s |

The 5 MiB run took 385.29 seconds including 58.49 seconds intentionally held. Pause, resume and the later 20 MiB stop were confirmed by the simulator. The 20 MiB fixture contains 640,457 commands; only the initial sample ran, so this is **not a full-job integrity or endurance pass**.

The simulator acknowledges at a target of 500 lines/s with a 1024-byte receive buffer. These rates show that the app could keep up with that workload; they are not measured maximum throughput or real machining time.

| Three baseline jobs | 469.9 MiB | 210.6 MiB | 4956.5 MiB |

| 20 MiB repeat | 1292.1 MiB | 462.3 MiB | 4100.1 MiB |

After the 5 MiB job was unloaded, the last recorded app PSS was 300.0 MiB; the last sample in the 20 MiB repeat was 337.9 MiB. Memory did come back down, but one cycle does not establish the absence of a slow leak. App-process measurements cover Android and embedded Node. WebView candidates are recorded separately; renderer ownership was not independently established, so these peaks are not silently added into one app total.

The first 20 MiB attempt crossed the conservative 1024 MiB app guard at 1081.0 MiB PSS. The benchmark intentionally force-stopped gSender after 3,252 acknowledgements. Android still reported 4427.0 MiB available. **This was a benchmark guard stop, not a spontaneous crash or evidence of a tablet memory limit.** The repeat used a 1536 MiB app guard and a 1536 MiB minimum-available-memory guard, after restarting gSender. It completed the planned sample without tripping either.

## Responsiveness and limitations

No spontaneous gSender crash or ANR was recorded in the successful captures; Android exit history was unchanged. During the baseline attempt to automate pause, Android’s UiAutomation helper failed with a `Bad file descriptor`; gSender continued. The pause/resume check was therefore performed in the 5 MiB run. One automated resume attempt did not reach the simulator; a later attempt did. These automation results do not provide a reliable finger-to-response latency measurement.

One immediate post-stop screenshot in the 20 MiB repeat still showed Running and older progress after the simulator had confirmed cancellation; a later fresh UI snapshot showed Idle. This warrants a focused UI-latency test at large file sizes. The duration of that display lag was not measured, and it should not be described as a whole-tablet freeze.

The original freeze was not reproduced. Tests beyond 20 MiB, a full 20 MiB run, repeated load/unload cycles, long thermal runs and the physical Android USB serial path remain untested. The K50 and K90 both kept close to the simulator target on the shared baseline, but differences in OS, display resolution, app warm-up and capture timing prevent a precise device-speed ranking.
