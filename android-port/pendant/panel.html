Uses gSender’s saved Precision feedrate. Tap the desired X, Y, Z, or STEP row to select it. All four rows stay visible. Turn STEP to edit the distance (0–10 mm, in 0.1 mm increments); this never moves the CNC. At STEP 0, axis turns do nothing. In Exact STEP mode, each accepted axis detent sends that finite distance. In Adaptive mode, isolated slow turns use STEP and sustained fast turning uses short variable-speed moves. Busy/late turns are discarded without disconnecting. Clicking does nothing.

10 mm means up to 10 mm of travel per accepted detent. Keep the machine clear and physical E-stop available. This is not an emergency-stop device. Reconnection never re-arms jogging. Keep gSender visible while jogging.
